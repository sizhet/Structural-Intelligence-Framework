This project follows the principles of [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) for readability. Release identifiers describe **repository publication milestones**, not demonstrated maturity, conformance, or deployment readiness of the proposed architecture.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-26

### Added — Research foundations

- `docs/foundations/SIF-001-From-Isolated-Intelligence-to-Open-Structural-Intelligence.md`
- `docs/foundations/SIF-002-Four-Functional-Domains-of-Structural-Intelligence.md`
- `docs/foundations/SIF-003-Structural-Precedents-and-Open-Intelligence-Evolution.md`

These manuscripts introduce SIF as an open, extensible research architecture rather than a closed or fully validated intelligence system.

### Added — Four functional domains

- `docs/functional-domains/SIF-004-Localization-and-Intelligence-Addressing.md`
- `docs/functional-domains/SIF-005-Per-Node-Intelligence-and-Multi-Perspective-Capabilities.md`
- `docs/functional-domains/SIF-006-Universal-Control-Planes-and-Scoring-Folding.md`
- `docs/functional-domains/SIF-007-Residual-Based-Structural-Growth.md`

The domains cover localization, heterogeneous per-node intelligence, scoring and control, and evidence-governed structural growth. They are **functional perspectives**, not mutually exclusive software layers.

### Added — Interoperability and structural precedent intelligence

- `docs/interoperability/SIF-009-Structural-Precedent-Registry.md`

### Scope and limitations

- This is a **research-framework and documentation release**, not a production software release.
- No executable reference implementation, interoperability certification, or independently reproduced experimental result is claimed by this changelog.
- Numerical improvements, emergence of higher-order capabilities, and worldview expansion are **research questions**, not established results.
- Scoring or successful evaluation does not itself grant operational authority.
- The initial publication is intentionally documentation-first; implementation and demonstrations are deferred pending specifications and validation.

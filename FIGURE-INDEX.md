## Recommended Repository Embedding Practice

Use paths relative to the file containing the reference:

- From the repository root: `figures/Fig-001-SIF-Grand-Architecture.png`
- From `docs/foundations/`, `docs/functional-domains/`, `docs/interoperability/`, `docs/validation-and-evolution/`, or `docs/frontiers/`: `../../figures/Fig-001-SIF-Grand-Architecture.png`
- From `specifications/` or `research/`: `../figures/Fig-001-SIF-Grand-Architecture.png`

For example, inside `docs/frontiers/SIF-015-Structural-Perception-Horizon-and-Worldview-Growth.md`:

```markdown
![Fig-008 — Perception Horizon and Worldview Growth](figures/Fig-008-Perception-Horizon-and-Worldview-Growth.png)

*Figure 8. Structural perception horizon and hypothesized worldview growth.*
```

For a figure reused across papers, keep the canonical asset in `figures/` and adapt the local explanatory caption rather than duplicating or renaming the image. The numbering `Fig-001` through `Fig-008` is repository-wide; an article may use a different local figure sequence if explicitly distinguished.

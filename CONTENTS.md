## XI. Research Status and Scope

The repository presents a **proposed architecture, conceptual analyses, candidate contracts, and a validation agenda**. It does not claim that all components have been implemented, that candidate interfaces are production-ready, or that frontier hypotheses have been empirically established. Where experiments contradict a proposal, the result should be recorded and the proposal revised or rejected.

The guiding principle is:

> **Standardize the contract, not the intelligence. Preserve the evidence, not merely the output. Govern the action, not the possibility of discovery.**
